# Evidence matrix · v1.9

This matrix separates normative positions from empirically testable claims. Citations are starting points, not endorsements of all claims made in Tabula Rasa. DOI metadata and applicability should be checked against the publisher before any external scholarly release.

## Source handling

- Distinguish peer-reviewed work, preprints, technical blogs, standards and normative argument.
- Do not convert conceptual arguments into quantified empirical certainty.
- Do not treat self-reports of experience as proof of consciousness.
- For every empirical experiment, record model/version, prompt, tools, authorization, environment, outcome, evaluator and uncertainty.
- A citation supports only the specific claim for which it is used.

## Evidence grading

**A:** multiple independent high-quality studies with convergent results; **B:** credible but limited replicated evidence; **C:** single study or relevant indirect evidence; **D:** theoretical, preliminary, preprint or expert proposal; **N:** normative claim, empirical grade not applicable. These are proposed categories, not completed evidence assessments.
