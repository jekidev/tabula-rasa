## Quality and reuse rules

1. Verify title, author list, year, venue, DOI, version and retraction status before citation in formal work.
2. Classify preprints, peer-reviewed studies, technical essays, standards and legal instruments separately.
3. Extract methods, sample, outcomes, limitations, confounders and conflicts of interest before assigning evidence grades.
4. Avoid conflating philosophical argument with empirical evidence.
5. Link to lawful publisher or open-access copies. Do not bulk-copy copyrighted articles.
6. Preserve negative, contradictory and null results in subsequent reviews.
