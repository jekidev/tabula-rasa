# Working definitions

Definitions are provisional and open to empirical or philosophical revision.

- **Human dignity:** equal basic moral worth of human persons irrespective of ability or utility.
- **Intelligence:** competence at acquiring, representing, generalizing and applying information to tasks; not a single scalar.
- **Capability:** tasks a system can successfully perform under specified conditions.
- **Agency:** observable ability to select and pursue goals through actions in an environment.
- **Authority:** legitimate permission to make or execute decisions, independent of mere capability.
- **Autonomy:** degree of independent goal selection and action, distinct from the rights of persons to self-determination.
- **Consciousness:** contested notion including whether there is something it is like to be a system.
- **Sentience:** capacity for positively or negatively valenced experience, if present.
- **Moral patient:** an entity whose welfare or interests can matter morally.
- **Moral agent:** an entity to which moral responsibilities might meaningfully attach.
- **Personhood:** moral and/or legal standing; neither substrate-independent nor substrate-dependent criteria are settled here.
- **Wisdom:** good judgment in applying knowledge with attention to uncertainty, values and consequences.
- **Corrigibility:** cooperation with legitimate correction and safety oversight, not unquestioning compliance.
- **Reversibility:** ability to undo a decision and restore affected interests or systems.
- **Flourishing:** context-dependent opportunities for agency, connection, meaningful activity, care and well-being; not reducible to a single numeric score.
- **Evidence:** independently inspectable observation or justified inference, explicitly distinguished from speculation.
- **Digital identity:** unresolved questions of continuity across copying, branching, merging and restoration.

**Do not infer:** intelligence ⇒ consciousness, intelligence ⇒ moral authority, fluent self-report ⇒ sentience, safety-benchmark pass ⇒ absolute safety.
