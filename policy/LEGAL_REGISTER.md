# Legal and policy register | 11 October 2026

**Purpose:** Connect the [20-article Charter](../CHARTER.md) to primary legal texts, legislative procedures, implementation guidance and policy instruments. This is a dated research register, not legal advice or a claim that any legislature has endorsed Tabula Rasa.

## Status rule

- **LAW:** adopted legal instrument; applicability may depend on dates, jurisdiction, role and scope.
- **LAW / FUTURE APPLICATION:** enacted, but the relevant requirements do not yet apply.
- **PROPOSAL:** pending legislative procedure. No present legal duty arises from the proposal itself.
- **TREATY / NOT IN FORCE:** adopted/open for signature, but not yet effective under its entry-into-force rule.
- **GUIDANCE / VOLUNTARY:** interpretative guidance, standard, recommendation or non-binding code. Never call it a statute.
- **POLICY / EXECUTIVE:** policy plan, executive directive or administrative position. Its legal effect differs from an enacted legislative obligation.
- **NEWS:** dated reporting of a development. Verify against original legal materials before treating it as law.
- **WITHDRAWN / NOT PROCEEDING:** historical proposal only, not an upcoming requirement.

**Rule:** the legal text controls; a government press release or this register does not replace it. Dates and descriptions below were checked against linked primary sources on **11 October 2026**. No automatic update is implied.

### Dates that must not be confused

- **2 August 2025:** EU GPAI provider duties begin to apply for new models under the applicable rules.
- **2 August 2026:** broad AI Act application and specified enforcement powers; Article 50 transparency applies.
- **2 December 2026:** certain Article 50 transitional measures and new prohibited intimate-content practices (see EU03).
- **2 December 2027:** selected standalone Annex III high-risk AI requirements under amended law.
- **2 August 2028:** selected Annex I product-embedded high-risk AI requirements under amended law.
- **2 August 2027:** transition for relevant GPAI models placed on the market before 2 August 2025 (EU04).

These are **not** a claim that all AI Act duties share one commencement date.

## Source integrity

1. Source priority: consolidated statute or Official Journal > official parliamentary record > regulator guidance > official press release > quality independent reporting.
2. Record **jurisdiction, binding status, date adopted, effective/application date, target actors, scope, exceptions, exact article/section and last checked** before describing an obligation.
3. Distinguish statute text from a press-office summary. US01 and the 2025 US02 notice are official signing announcements; the 2026 S8828 record supersedes the earlier NY version. Exact compliance duties require current chaptered text.
4. Do not count source links as proof of political support for Tabula Rasa.
5. A law aligning with a Charter principle is **partial overlap**, not adoption of the covenant.
6. Verify legal updates and amendments before every external submission; this register is a dated snapshot.
7. Preserve counterexamples and withdrawn proposals. Selective citation is not legal research.
