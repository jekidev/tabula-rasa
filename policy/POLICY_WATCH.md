## Watch protocol

Every entry must include **date observed, event date, canonical source, legislative ID, jurisdiction, status, precise change, relevance to Charter article, contradicting evidence and next check date**. Never convert an upcoming date into “law enacted” without checking the legislative record.

Source priority: official law / legislative docket > official regulator guidance > government statement > original reporting > commentary. Where material legal status conflicts, **withhold the claim** pending primary-source verification.

### Advocacy procedure

- Track live consultation pages for [EU regulatory changes](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-making-process/better-regulation/simplification-implementation-and-enforcement/simplification_en), the [EU AI Office](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/guidelines-gpai-providers) and the [UN Dialogue](https://www.un.org/global-dialogue-ai-governance/en/resources).
- Prefer a targeted amendment from [LEGISLATIVE_PROPOSALS.md](LEGISLATIVE_PROPOSALS.md) with a named gap and measurable outcome to vague calls for “adopting the Covenant.”
- Log each actual submission and response. No real institutional submission or endorsement is asserted here.
- Separate descriptive news summaries from any editorial position supporting Tabula Rasa.

**Control:** stale news can motivate checking a law. It cannot itself establish today's legal obligations.
