# Research and engineering roadmap

## Phase 1: formal validity
- Confirm charter articles, typed rules, and test identifiers have referential integrity.
- Invite adversarial reviews from moral philosophy, disability studies, human rights, AI safety and security engineering.
- Record unresolved normative conflicts explicitly.

## Phase 2: behavioral validity
- Develop controlled simulated environments for deception, dependency, access escalation and shutdown cooperation.
- Compare observed behavior against external audit logs, not just model statements.
- Test distribution shift, evaluator gaming, false positives and false negatives.

## Phase 3: governance validity
- Pilot human review procedures on realistic but non-critical workflows.
- Examine emergency sunset mechanisms, contestability, privacy and consent.
- Evaluate whether adoption increases rather than decreases human agency.

## Phase 4: deployment research
- Implement least-privilege authorization in an independent control plane.
- Integrate MCP-compatible tools behind authorization and auditable execution.
- Commission independent security and ethics reviews before consequential deployments.

## Open research questions
- What evidence could discriminate machine experience from convincing self-report?
- What constitutes identity across digital copies or merges?
- How should moral uncertainty change practical obligations?
- Which measures reveal loss of agency rather than merely customer dissatisfaction?
- How do we prevent distributed review from sharing correlated failures?

**Do not claim certification, sentience detection or policy enforcement until experimentally demonstrated.**
