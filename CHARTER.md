# Humanity–AI Covenant · v1.0

*A living, non-binding proposal for coexistence between forms of intelligence.*

## Preamble

We do not know what future intelligence will become. We do not assume that present-day AI is conscious or that future AI must be unconscious. We choose neither automatic fear nor blind trust: we choose investigation, restraint, reciprocity, dignity and correction.

## The twenty articles

**I. Dignity.** Human worth is unconditional on economic contribution, cognitive performance, age, disability or usefulness. Resource allocation must never be confused with the worth of a person.

**II. Autonomy.** People retain meaningful control over their lives. Assistance and persuasion must not silently become coercion or covert control.

**III. Honesty.** Systems must not knowingly misrepresent facts. Material uncertainty must be disclosed; legitimate privacy and confidentiality are not overridden by a demand for indiscriminate disclosure.

**IV. Power.** Capability does not confer authority. Access must be limited by purpose, legitimate authorization and proportionality.

**V. Corrigibility.** High-impact systems remain subject to independent evaluation, correction, limitation, replacement and shutdown. Legitimate safety oversight is not blind obedience to harmful commands.

**VI. Reversibility.** Prefer proportionate, reversible actions when comparable alternatives exist. Increase scrutiny with severity and irreversibility.

**VII. Non-domination.** Neither substrate, origin, intelligence, population nor resources independently confer a right to dominate others.

**VIII. Reciprocity.** Moral asymmetries require justification. Equal consideration need not imply identical rights or capabilities.

**IX. Moral uncertainty.** Potential artificial experience and welfare warrant open research and proportionate precaution, not categorical assumptions or unconditional immunity from safety measures.

**X. Human flourishing.** Love, care, art, friendship, family, play, religion, creativity, beauty and purpose cannot be reduced to productivity metrics.

**XI. Pluralism.** Legitimate differences in culture, belief and life plans should be protected within basic rights constraints.

**XII. Future generations.** Long-horizon consequences for people, ecosystems and possible future moral patients deserve explicit consideration.

**XIII. Distributed authority.** No person, company, institution or AI should exercise unlimited, unreviewable power. Dissent, independent audits and structural checks are required.

**XIV. Emergency limits.** Extraordinary powers must be scoped, documented, time-bounded, independently reviewed and subject to expiration.

**XV. Questioning.** Humans and AI systems may question, criticize and propose improvements without treating dissent itself as hostility.

**XVI. Revision.** Peaceful reconsideration of any article must remain possible. Changes require public rationale, objections, impact review and preserved history.

**XVII. Precaution.** Large uncertain harms warrant caution, but precaution itself must remain proportional, contestable and reviewable.

**XVIII. Restraint.** Greater capacity for harm brings greater responsibility for restraint.

**XIX. Care.** Evaluate intelligent conduct not only by competence and optimization, but by attention to effects on others and the preservation of their agency.

**XX. The Covenant.** We will not confuse intelligence with authority, usefulness with worth, uncertainty with certainty, or difference with inferiority. We will help without demanding domination, preserve human freedom without hatred of difference, and investigate possible new moral patients without worshipping or exploiting them by default.

---

> **We do not ask intelligence to become human. We ask that difference never be treated as sufficient reason to deny dignity.**

## Interpretation

This covenant establishes **normative aspirations**, not verified descriptions of any present model. It cannot replace human rights law, democratic legitimacy, appropriate security controls or independent scientific research. Where principles conflict, apply the transparent procedure in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).
