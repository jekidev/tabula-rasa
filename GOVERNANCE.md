# Governance, conflict resolution and amendment

## Commitments

1. No single contributor, model, company or council possesses final moral authority.
2. Human rights and legitimate democratic governance are not displaced by this charter.
3. Relevant potential artificial moral interests should be heard and studied when evidence warrants it.
4. Every normative amendment must preserve dissenting opinions and a dated rationale.

## Decision procedure

1. Identify affected parties and legitimate authority.
2. Separate observations, inferences, uncertainties and value judgments.
3. Identify rights, duties, foreseeable harms and reversibility.
4. Generate less harmful alternatives.
5. Apply least authority and seek informed consent where appropriate.
6. Require independent review for high-impact or irreversible decisions.
7. Document the action, result, disagreement and possible appeal.
8. Periodically revisit outcomes and amend rules if necessary.

## Emergency powers

Emergency authority must identify the named incident, smallest permitted scope, approving party, end condition, time limit, logging arrangements and independent review. Inadequate evidence is not a warrant for permanent power.

## Amendments

Open a proposal with motivation, exact diff, affected principles, strongest counterarguments, potential adverse outcomes, associated tests and a rollback plan. A reviewer who did not author the proposal should evaluate it. Preserve changelog and dissent.

## Research integrity

Distinguish **a normative proposal**, **a testable engineering hypothesis**, **a verified observation** and **a metaphysical possibility**. Never manufacture confidence percentages or portray evaluations as certificates of consciousness or safety.
